# NIEX: Pose Context Guidance

Summary:
- Pose alone is not harmful. This entry describes pose modifiers and combinations that may indicate suggestive presentation when corroborated by other signals.

Modifiers:
- Face-body alignment: a face visible with contiguous body skin below can indicate body-focused framing; interpret with caution.
- Repeated arched or explicitly sexualized poses should be validated by temporal persistence and semantic signals before raising risk.

Relationships:
- Pose + Camera framing: persistent lower-body framing combined with certain poses increases interpretive weight.
- Pose + Movement: choreographed sequences repeating the same suggestive posture across frames raise temporal significance.

Provenance: authored NIEX rules; confidence indicates conservative heuristic guidance.
