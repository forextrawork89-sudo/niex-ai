# Pose Category Context

Summary:
- Pose category should not be treated as harmful by itself. It is a contextual cue that becomes meaningful only when combined with movement, clothing, framing, temporal persistence, or text intent.

Categories:
- standing: neutral everyday posture; low risk unless combined with persistent framing or explicit intent.
- sitting: ordinary social or interview posture; low risk unless used in a repeated suggestive pattern.
- lying: ordinary rest or medical/athletic posture; neutral unless paired with exploitative framing.
- bending: often natural movement; can be neutral or editorial depending on context.
- dancing/movement: choreography or performance; not inherently harmful. Treat as higher-risk only with repeated suggestive framing or explicit text intent.
- athletic/action: sports or exercise; typically safe with sportswear or training context.
- fashion/editorial: posed presentation; safe in fashion or commercial editorial settings unless combined with explicit sexualized framing.
- neutral/social: everyday social poses; weak evidence by themselves.
- ambiguous: weak or incomplete visual evidence; remain uncertain.

Interpretation rule:
- Single-frame pose category alone should not trigger a harmful conclusion.
- Repeated pose categories across multiple frames can modestly increase confidence when combined with temporal evidence.
