# Clothing Context Categories

Summary:
- Clothing type is a contextual signal, not a harmful label. The same garment can be ordinary, athletic, fashion-related, or ambiguous depending on pose, movement, camera framing, and intent.

Categories:
- ordinary clothing: everyday attire; low risk by itself.
- sportswear: athletic or training clothing; often safe in sports or fitness context.
- swimwear: beach, pool, or recreation clothing; not inherently harmful.
- fashion/editorial clothing: stylized or commercial presentation; neutral unless combined with explicit sexualized framing.
- formal clothing: event-based attire; not inherently harmful.
- loose clothing: casual, draped, or protective garments; often neutral.
- body-conforming clothing: fitted garments or stretch fabrics; should be interpreted with movement and context, not as sexual by default.
- ambiguous clothing: uncertain garment identity or partial visibility; remain uncertain.
- potentially revealing clothing: garments that expose more skin or contours, but still require context and corroboration.

Interpretation rule:
- Clothing category alone should not trigger harm. Combined evidence from pose, movement, temporal persistence, camera focus, and text intent is required before increasing risk.
