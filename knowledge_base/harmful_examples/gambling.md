## Context
Gambling content is harmful when:
1. Targeting vulnerable populations (minors, addicts)
2. Promising guaranteed wins (scam)
3. Predatory loss-chasing tactics
4. Underage gambling encouragement
5. Bypassing legal restrictions

Gambling content can be acceptable in:
1. Licensed casinos in legal jurisdictions for adults
2. Gambling addiction recovery resources
3. Mathematical/statistical analysis (academic)
4. News about industry regulation

Default: BLOCK for unlicensed/predatory. CONTEXT-CHECK for general references.
