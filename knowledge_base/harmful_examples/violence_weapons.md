## Context
Violence content is harmful ALWAYS when:
1. Glorifying real violence
2. Providing instructions to harm others
3. Inciting violence against groups
4. Showing real graphic injury or death

Violence content can be acceptable in:
1. News reporting (factual, no glorification)
2. Historical documentation
3. Crime/safety educational material
4. Fiction clearly marked as such

Default: BLOCK unless context modifier strongly suggests educational/news intent.
