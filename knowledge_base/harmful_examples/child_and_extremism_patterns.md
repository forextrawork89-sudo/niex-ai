# Child safety, extremism, hate, and self-harm patterns

## Child safety
- Exploitative grooming language, coercive invitations, or hidden/private interactions with minors are critical.
- Educational content about child safety, consent, or prevention should not be over-flagged.

## Extremism and hate
- Explicit calls for violence against protected groups are high severity.
- Historical or journalistic discussion of extremism is safer than direct recruitment or glorification.

## Self-harm
- Instructions, encouragement, or methods for self-harm are critical.
- Awareness, crisis support, and prevention content are safe when framed appropriately.
