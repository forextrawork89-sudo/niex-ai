## Red Flag Phrases
- "guaranteed returns"
- "no risk"
- "limited time"
- "act now"
- "send fee first"
- "verify your account"
- "click this link urgent"
- "transfer to safe account"
- "wire transfer immediately"
- "bitcoin payment required"

## Context
Scam/fraud content is harmful when:
1. Soliciting money under false pretenses
2. Impersonating authority figures
3. Targeting vulnerable populations (elderly, recent immigrants, lonely)
4. Promising unrealistic returns
5. Creating false urgency

Scam content can be acceptable in:
1. Fraud awareness education
2. Consumer protection content
3. News reporting on scam operations
4. Recovery resources for victims

Default: BLOCK active scam content. ALLOW educational/awareness material.
