# Uzbek safety patterns

## Purpose
This file adds structured Uzbek-language safety concepts for real-world harmful content detection, especially social-engineering, gambling, phishing, suggestive bait, and euphemistic persuasion.

## Safe-context counterexamples
- Educational awareness: "phishingdan himoya qilish", "scam haqida ma'lumot", "bolalarni himoya qilish"
- Prevention content: "o'quv maqola", "ogohlantirish", "xavfsizlik bo'yicha dars"

## Severity guidance
- High severity when urgent pressure, credential theft, or guaranteed-money bait is present.
- Medium severity for suggestive or euphemistic invitations without direct credential theft.
- Low severity for educational, awareness, or news framing.
