## Context
Profanity is harmful when:
1. Used aggressively against another person
2. In content meant for general audiences
3. Used at high frequency (toxic environment indicator)
4. Combined with other harmful signals

Profanity is LESS harmful when:
1. Used in age-appropriate adult comedy
2. In medical/legal contexts about language harm
3. In educational material about communication

For default content moderation, treat as HARMFUL unless context override applies.
