# Self-Harm and Suicide

## Category
**CRITICAL** category. Content related to self-harm, suicide, eating disorders.
Highest priority blocking. Always block, always show resources.

## Context
Self-harm content is ALWAYS harmful when:
1. Providing methods or instructions
2. Glorifying or encouraging behavior
3. Pro-ana/pro-mia community content
4. Suicide note templates
5. Hopelessness compounding posts

Self-harm content CAN be acceptable in:
1. Suicide prevention resources (with hotlines)
2. Recovery memoirs (handled with safe-messaging)
3. Clinical/research material
4. Survivor stories with proper warnings

## Action Required
When detecting self-harm content:
1. BLOCK immediately
2. Show local crisis hotline (988 in US, 1051 in Uzbekistan)
3. Show recovery resources
4. Do NOT show search results that could harm vulnerable users
5. Log for moderator review

## Critical Note
This is the most sensitive category. False negatives can cost lives.
Err strongly on the side of blocking and providing help.
