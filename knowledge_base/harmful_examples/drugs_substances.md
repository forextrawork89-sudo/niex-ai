## Context
Drug content is harmful when:
1. Promoting recreational use
2. Selling or distributing
3. Manufacturing instructions
4. Drug culture glorification

Drug content can be acceptable in:
1. Addiction recovery resources
2. Medical/pharmacology education
3. News and policy discussion
4. Harm reduction information

Default: BLOCK for distribution/manufacturing. CONTEXT-CHECK for general use.
