# Movement Category Context

Summary:
- Movement category should be interpreted as a contextual clue. Ordinary movement, sports movement, dance, fashion posing, and ambiguous movement are all neutral possibilities until corroborated by other signals.

Categories:
- ordinary movement: walking, turning, talking, gesturing; low risk by itself.
- sports movement: training, exercise, sprinting, stretching; often safe with sports context.
- choreography/dance: performance, rehearsal, dance content; not inherently harmful.
- fashion posing: edited or stylized posing; should be interpreted with fashion/editorial context.
- ambiguous movement: unclear visual evidence or low-confidence motion; remain uncertain.
- potentially suggestive movement: repeated body-focused motion or framing; only increase risk when paired with pose, clothing, temporal patterns, or explicit intent.

Interpretation rule:
- Movement alone is insufficient. Repeated movement over time plus framing or text intent is required for stronger risk conclusions.
