# NIEX: Facial & Emotional Context Guidance

Summary:
- Facial expressions are contextual modifiers. Smiles, attractiveness, or neutral expressions are not harmful unless paired with other corroborating signals.

Guidance:
- Emotional indicators should be combined with pose, movement, and semantic intent before influencing decisions.
- Distress, coercion, or fear expressions are high-priority safety signals and should be escalated to human review.
