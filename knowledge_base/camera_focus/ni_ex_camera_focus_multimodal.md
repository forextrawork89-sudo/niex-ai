# NIEX: Camera Framing and Focus Guidance

Summary:
- Camera framing modifies meaning: persistent tight framing on specific body regions increases interpretive weight only when corroborated by movement/pose/text signals.

Key heuristics:
- Face-center persistence suggests subject-focused framing; pair with body-region focus to evaluate intent.
- Lower-body persistent framing should not by itself be treated as harmful but is a relevant context modifier.
- Rapid zoom/crop patterns with increasing skin percentage across frames are stronger signals than single-frame crops.
