# Uzbek Social-Media Context

Summary:
- Uzbek social-media language should be interpreted contextually. Words or phrases may be neutral, playful, or manipulative depending on surrounding intent, repeated patterns, and multimodal evidence.

Patterns:
- Mixed Uzbek/Russian/English posts often use slang, transliteration, or abbreviations that are not inherently sexual.
- Suggestive bait or persuasion language becomes more meaningful when paired with repeated framing, camera focus, or clothing/pose context.
- Transliteration and misspellings should be treated as weak evidence unless part of an explicit pattern.

Interpretation rule:
- Do not equate slang or transliteration with harmful intent by itself.
- Increase confidence only when a multilingual phrase is repeated with visual or temporal indicators.
